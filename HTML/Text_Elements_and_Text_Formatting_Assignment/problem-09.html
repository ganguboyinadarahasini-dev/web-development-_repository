GLA University - Student Code of Conduct & Important Notices

Academic Year 2026-27 | Applicable to all B.Tech Students

General Rules

1. All students must carry their valid college ID card inside the campus at all times.
Entry without ID card is strictly prohibited at the main gate and library. Students will not be allowed to enter labs without ID card.

2. Use of mobile phones is strictly prohibited inside classrooms and laboratories during lectures.
If found using mobile phones, the device will be confiscated. Please keep your phones on silent mode to avoid disturbance.

Attendance

3. Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory in every subject to be eligible for End Semester Examination.
Students with attendance below 75% will be detained. Medical leave is only valid with a proper medical certificate submitted within 3 days.

4. Attendance for Web Development Lab: Attendance below 75% will lead to detention and you will have to repeat the lab in next semester. Regular attendance will be taken at the beginning of each lab.

Assignment Submission

5. All assignments must be submitted on the university portal before the deadline.
Late submissions will not be accepted through email or hard copy. File should be in PDF format and named as RollNo_Name_AssignmentNo.

6. HTML Text Elements Assignment Deadline: Last date for submission is 10th September 2026, 11:59 PM.
Penalty of 5 marks per day will be applied for late submission after 10th September 2026. Submit Problem 1 to Problem 9 as separate HTML files in a single ZIP folder.

Examination

7. Students must report to the examination hall at least 30 minutes before the start of the examination.
No student will be allowed to enter after 15 minutes of commencement. Please bring your admit card and ID card without fail.

8. Use of any unfair means, electronic gadgets, or copying in the examination is a punishable offense.
Any student found guilty will get zero marks in that paper and suspension for one semester. This rule is applicable for both Mid-Term and End-Term exams.

Discipline

9. Ragging in any form is strictly banned inside and outside the campus as per UGC guidelines.
Any student found involved in ragging will face immediate rustication from the university and legal action. Freshers are advised to report any such incident to the Anti-Ragging Committee immediately.

10. Students must maintain cleanliness and discipline in the campus, canteen and hostel premises.
Damaging college property will lead to a fine. Help us keep the campus clean and green.

Important Note

All students are advised to check the official university website and notice board regularly for updates. Ignorance of rules will not be considered as an excuse. For any queries, contact your class coordinator.
