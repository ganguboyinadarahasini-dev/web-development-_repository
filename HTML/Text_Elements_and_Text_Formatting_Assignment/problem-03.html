ISRO Successfully Launches Chandrayaan-4 Mission to Explore Lunar South Pole

Mission aims to collect new soil samples and search for water ice with advanced robotic technology

In a historic achievement for India's space program, the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has successfully launched the Chandrayaan-4 mission from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota. The launch took place on 5th September 2026 at 6:15 AM IST, marking a major milestone in lunar exploration. The heavy-lift LVM3 rocket carried the orbiter, lander, and rover modules and placed them precisely into the intended orbit. Scientists at the mission control center celebrated as telemetry confirmed a perfect launch.

The primary objective of this mission is to explore the largely uncharted south pole region of the Moon. According to ISRO officials, the rover will drill up to 1 meter deep to collect lunar soil and analyze it for traces of water ice and essential minerals. This data is considered crucial for future long-term human missions to the Moon. The mission also carries six advanced scientific payloads from India, USA, and Japan to study lunar topography, mineralogy, and the thin exosphere. The lander is expected to make a soft landing on September 28, 2026.

The Prime Minister congratulated the ISRO team and said, this success belongs to every Indian and shows what we can achieve with dedication and innovation. The entire nation is proud of our scientists. The mission budget is reported to be around Rs. 1,200 crores and is seen as one of the most cost-effective lunar missions in the world. Citizens across the country watched the live telecast with great excitement. ISRO will now perform a series of orbit-raising maneuvers over the next three weeks before the lander attempts the final descent in the Lunar South Pole region.

Delhi Government Announces Free Laptop Scheme for College Students

Scheme to benefit over 2 lakh students from government colleges starting this academic year

The Delhi Government has announced a new welfare initiative to distribute free laptops to students enrolled in government colleges and universities. The announcement was made by the Education Minister on 4th September 2026 at a press conference in Delhi Secretariat. Under this scheme, all students who scored above 75% in their previous academic year will be eligible to receive a laptop with pre-installed educational software.

According to the official notification, more than 2,00,000 students will receive high-performance laptops in the first phase of distribution by November 2026. The government has allocated Rs. 400 crores for this project. The aim is to promote digital education and bridge the digital divide among students from economically weaker sections. The distribution will begin from the North Campus, University of Delhi and will be completed within 60 days.

Students and parents have welcomed this decision with great enthusiasm. The laptops will come with 1 year of free internet data and access to online courses from platforms like Swayam and NPTEL. The application process will start online from next week through the official education department portal. This step is expected to significantly improve the quality of higher education and help students learn new skills.
