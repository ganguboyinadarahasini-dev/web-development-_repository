Post 1: My Journey into Programming - How I Started Coding

I started learning programming in my first year of college with C language. Initially, it was very confusing to understand loops and pointers, but with regular practice I started enjoying it. Now, I love building websites and solving problems on LeetCode. Programming is not just about writing code, it's about solving real-world problems logically. It has taught me patience and analytical thinking. If you are a beginner, I would suggest you start with Python or C and practice daily. Remember, consistency is more important than speed.

Post 2: Technology - Why Artificial Intelligence is the Future

Artificial Intelligence is transforming every industry from healthcare to education. Tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Muse are making our work faster and smarter. As a Computer Science student, I am very excited about AI and Machine Learning. AI will not replace humans, but humans who know how to use AI will replace those who don't. Currently, I am learning about Large Language Models and neural networks. I believe learning AI skills in 2026 is as important as learning computer basics was in 2000.

Post 3: Education - How I Manage College Studies Effectively

Managing college studies along with coding practice is not easy. I follow a simple method: I make daily to-do lists and keep 2 hours every evening for self-study. I also make short notes after every lecture which helps during exams. Revision is the key to scoring good marks in semester exams. I try to avoid last-minute preparation and instead revise regularly. My advice to all juniors is to focus on understanding concepts rather than just mugging up for exams, because that knowledge will help you in interviews.

Post 4: Travel - My First Solo Trip to Manali

Last December, I went on my first solo trip to Manali, Himachal Pradesh. It was one of the best experiences of my life. The snow-covered mountains, the cold breeze and the peaceful environment made me feel very calm. I stayed in a small homestay near Old Manali and met many travelers from different countries. Traveling solo taught me to be independent and confident. I trekked to Jogini Waterfall and also visited Solang Valley. This trip taught me that sometimes you need to go out of your comfort zone to learn more about yourself.

Post 5: Career - How to Prepare for Placements in Final Year

Placement preparation is a major concern for every final year student. From my experience and seniors' guidance, I have learned that there are three main pillars: Data Structures & Algorithms, Core Subjects, and Projects. I practice 2-3 DSA problems daily on LeetCode and revise DBMS, OS, and CN weekly. Having at least 2 strong projects on your resume is mandatory to get shortlisted. Also, don't ignore communication skills and aptitude preparation. Start early, be consistent, and don't compare your journey with others. Your hard work will definitely pay off.

Post 6: Bonus - My Favorite Books for Self Growth

Apart from technical books, I love reading self-help and motivational books. My favorite book is "Atomic Habits" by James Clear. It taught me how small habits can lead to big results. I also liked "Deep Work" by Cal Newport which explains the importance of focused work without distractions. Reading for 30 minutes daily has improved my vocabulary and concentration. I would highly recommend every student to develop a reading habit along with their technical studies.
