GLA University

About the College

Ganeshi Lal Agarwal University, formerly known as GLA Institute of Technology, is one of the most prestigious engineering institutions in India. Established in 2010, it has a rich history of academic excellence and innovation. Located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, the university is spread over a lush green campus of 164 acres. GLA University is known for its highly qualified faculty, state-of-the-art laboratories, and strong industry connections. The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate in various fields of engineering, science, and management.

Courses Offered

GLA University offers a wide range of courses to cater to the diverse interests of students. The major programs include Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Engineering, Information Technology, Software Engineering, Electronics and Communication, Mechanical, Electrical, and Civil Engineering. It also offers Master of Technology (M.Tech), Master of Business Administration (MBA), Bachelor of Design (B.Des), and Ph.D. programs. The curriculum is regularly updated to meet the current industry standards and includes practical training, projects, and internships to ensure holistic development of students.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University is vibrant, engaging, and full of opportunities. The university has numerous technical and cultural societies like E-Cell, Robotics Society, Music Society, and Dramatics Society. Our annual cultural festival 'Engifest' and technical festival 'Invictus' are among the biggest college festivals in North India. The campus provides excellent facilities including a central library, sports complex, gymnasium, hostels, canteens, and innovation labs. Students enjoy a perfect balance between academics and extracurricular activities, which helps in overall personality development.
