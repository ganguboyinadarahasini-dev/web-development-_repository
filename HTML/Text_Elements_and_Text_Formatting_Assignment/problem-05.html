Science Notes - Quick Reference Sheet

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter, its properties and reactions. An atom is the smallest unit of a chemical element that retains its chemical properties.

Important Chemical Formulas:

1. Water - H2O - Water is essential for all known forms of life.

2. Carbon Dioxide - CO2 - Produced during respiration and combustion.

3. Oxygen - O2 - Oxygen is required for breathing and combustion.

4. Sulphuric Acid - H2SO4 - A strong acid used in batteries and industry.

5. Ammonia - NH3 - Used in fertilizers and cleaning products.

6. Glucose - C6H12O6 - Main source of energy for living organisms.

7. Calcium Carbonate - CaCO3 - Found in chalk, limestone and marble.

8. Reaction: 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O

Physics

Physics deals with matter, energy, motion and force. Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, it can only be transformed from one form to another.

1. Area: Unit of area is square meter - m2. Formula: Area of square = side2 = a2. Area is a scalar quantity.

2. Volume: Unit of volume is cubic meter - m3. Volume of cube = a3. Volume measures space occupied.

3. Einstein's Mass-Energy Relation: E = mc2 where c is speed of light (3 x 108 m/s).

4. Newton's Second Law: Force = mass x acceleration, F = ma. Unit is Newton.

5. Kinetic Energy: KE = (1/2)mv2 where m is mass and v is velocity in m/s. Power of velocity is 2.

6. Acceleration due to gravity: g = 9.8 m/s2. The value of g is approximately 9.8 m/s2 on Earth surface.

7. Electric Power: P = I2R where I is current and R is resistance.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms. Cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

1. Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food. Equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

2. Respiration: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + Energy. Respiration releases energy from glucose.

3. Human Blood Groups: A, B, AB, O. Water content in blood plasma is about 90% H2O.

4. DNA - Deoxyribonucleic Acid carries genetic information. CO2 level affects breathing rate.
