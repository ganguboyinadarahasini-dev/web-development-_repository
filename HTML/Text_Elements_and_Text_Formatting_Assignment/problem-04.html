Mathematics Formula Sheet

Algebra

1. Square of sum of two terms: (a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2

2. Square of difference of two terms: (a - b)2 = a2 - 2ab + b2

3. Sum of squares: a2 + b2 = (a + b)2 - 2ab

4. Difference of squares: a2 - b2 = (a + b)(a - b)

5. Cube of sum: (a + b)3 = a3 + b3 + 3ab(a + b)

6. Quadratic Formula: For ax2 + bx + c = 0, x = [-b ± √(b2 - 4ac)] / 2a

Geometry

7. Area of a Circle: A = πr2 where r is the radius of the circle.

8. Circumference of a Circle: C = 2πr

9. Pythagoras Theorem: In a right-angled triangle, a2 + b2 = c2, where c is the hypotenuse.

10. Equation of a line: y = mx + c, where m is slope. If point is (x1, y1), equation is y - y1 = m(x - x1)

Mensuration

11. Volume of a Cube: V = a3 where a is the side of the cube.

12. Volume of a Sphere: V = (4/3)πr3

13. Total Surface Area of a Cylinder: A = 2πr(r + h) where r is radius and h is height.

14. Volume of a Cylinder: V = πr2h

15. Area of Triangle with coordinates: If vertices are A(x1, y1), B(x2, y2), C(x3, y3), then Area = (1/2)|x1(y2 - y3) + x2(y3 - y1) + x3(y1 - y2)|
