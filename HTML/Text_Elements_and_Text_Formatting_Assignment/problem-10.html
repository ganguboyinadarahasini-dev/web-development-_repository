About University

Ganeshi Lal Agarwal University, commonly known as GLA, is one of themost prestigious engineering universities in India. It was established in 2010. The university is located in Mathura,Delhi Road , Uttar Pradesh.

GLA is ranked among the top 10 engineering colleges in India for its academic excellence and placement record. The university has a sprawling campus of 164 acres with state-of-the-art infrastructure. Our mission is to foster innovation, research and entrepreneurship among students and to produce skilled engineers who can contribute to nation building. The university offers a vibrant student life with more than 50 technical and cultural societies.

Departments

GLA has 15 academic departments that offer a wide range of courses in engineering, technology and management. Each department has highly qualified faculty members and well-equipped laboratories for research and development. The university focuses on interdisciplinary learning and practical exposure.

Major departments include Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Civil Engineering. The Department of Computer Science is the most sought-after department with highest placement packages up to 65Lakh per annum.Every department has a separate building with an area of more than 5000 m2 and volume of laboratories exceeding 15000 m3.

Academic Programs

GLA offers various undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs. At the undergraduate level, the university offers Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in 15 specializations with a duration of 4 years. The admission to B.Tech is done through JEE Mains examination followed by JAC Delhi counselling. The university has more than 3000 seats for B.Tech programs.

At the postgraduate level, we offer M.Tech, MBA, and M.Sc programs. The M.Tech program is of 2 years and admission is through GATE score. Our Ph.D program is highly research oriented where scholars work on cutting-edge technologies. The curriculum is designed as per NEP 2020 and includes subjects like Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Data Science. Students learn programming where complexity is often calculated as O(n2) for nested loops and algorithms like (a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2 are used in logical building. All programs follow a semester system with continuous evaluation.

Campus Facilities

DTU provides world-class facilities to its students to ensure a comfortable and enriching college life. The university has a central library with more than 1,50,000 books, e-journals and digital resources. The library is fully air-conditioned and open from 9 AM to 8 PM on all working days. There are separate hostels for boys and girls with capacity for over 2000 students.

Other facilities include a fully Wi-Fi enabled campus, 10 computer centers, sports complex with cricket ground, football ground, basketball courts, and an open-air theatre. The university also has a health center, shopping complex, and 24x7 canteen facility. Entrepreneurship and innovation are promoted through DTU's Incubation Centre (DTU-IIF) which has incubated more than 100 startups. The campus also has a knowledge park of 105 sq. ft. dedicated to research.
