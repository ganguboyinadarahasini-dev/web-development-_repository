University Admission Handbook

Welcome to the GLA University Admission Handbook. This guide provides essential information for prospective students.

Admission Overview

GLA University welcomes applications from students who meet the eligibility requirements for undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

"Our admission office is committed to making the application process clear, fair, and accessible to every student."

Eligibility

Applicants must have completed the required qualifying education and satisfy the academic requirements of their chosen program.

Mandatory requirement: Applicants must submit all required academic documents before the application can be processed.

Application deadline: 30 June 2026

Application Process

  1. Choose a program or course.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload all required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application before the deadline.
  7. Wait for the admission decision.
  8. Complete registration after acceptance.

Required Documents

  • Academic certificates and mark sheets
  • Proof of identity
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Transfer or migration certificate, if applicable
  • Proof of address
  • Entrance examination score, if required

Important Terms

Eligibility
The academic and other conditions an applicant must satisfy.
Merit List
A list of applicants ranked according to their admission performance.
Counselling
A process through which eligible students select available programs.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on specified criteria.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling as a university student.
