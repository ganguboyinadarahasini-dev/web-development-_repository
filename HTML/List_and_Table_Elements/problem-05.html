Registration Process

  1. Visit the official portal or click the banner link above.
  2. Fill out the participant details accurately.
  3. Select the desired event categories.
  4. Pay the registration fee online before October 15, 2026.

Fest Rules

  • College ID is mandatory for all participants at entry.
  • Alcohol, smoking, and illegal substances are strictly prohibited.
  • Participants must arrive at least 15 minutes prior to their event schedule.
  • Decision of the judges will be final and binding.

Event Terminology

Open Event
An event open to participants from any stream or department without prerequisite requirements.
Team Event
A competition requiring a team of 2 to 5 members to participate jointly.
Individual Event
A single-player competition evaluating individual skill.
Registration
The mandatory formal sign-up process required prior to participating in any event.
Coordinator
A designated faculty member or student manager responsible for overseeing a specific event category.
Final Round
The ultimate stage of a competition featuring the top qualifiers from preliminary rounds.
