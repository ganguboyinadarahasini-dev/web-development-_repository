Welcome to CityCare Clinic

CityCare is a multi-specialty healthcare facility located in the heart of the city, dedicated to providing accessible, affordable and quality healthcare. We serve over 10,000 patients monthly with a team of 50+ specialists.

Our clinic was formerly known as City Health Center CityCare Multi-Specialty Clinic after expansion in 2020. We now offer advanced diagnostics including H2O-based hydration therapy and E=mc2 inspired precision radiology.

Understanding Appointment Types

Consultation
First-time visit where doctor evaluates your health concerns and advises next steps.
Follow-up
Subsequent visit to review reports or track progress. Bring previous prescription.
OPD
Outpatient Department – General visits that do not require hospital admission.
Appointment
A pre-scheduled time slot reserved for you to avoid waiting.

How to Book an Appointment

  1. Select your Department and preferred Doctor
  2. Choose your preferred Date and Time slot
  3. Fill the Appointment Request Form below
  4. You will receive SMS/email confirmation within 2 hours
  5. Arrive 15 minutes early on appointment day
