  1. Review degree program details and eligibility prerequisites.
  2. Fill out the online Apply Now application form below.
  3. Upload required transcripts, ID proofs, and recommendation letters.
  4. Pay the non-refundable application fee securely online.
  5. Track your application status using your generated reference code.

Key Terms Defined

Eligibility
The minimum academic qualification and criteria required for admission into a specific degree course.
Merit List
A ranked listing of applicants generated based on academic marks and entrance examination performance.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to deserving students based on merit, financial need, or specific talents.
Application Fee
A standard processing fee required at the time of form submission to evaluate candidate credentials.
