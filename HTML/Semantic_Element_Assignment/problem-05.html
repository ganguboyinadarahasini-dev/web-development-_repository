Event Overview

Welcome to Bharat's Largest Career Festival

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a 3-day mega event bringing students, graduates, job seekers under one roof. Objective: education to employability.

Organized by NSDC & AICTE, formerly Rozgar Mela 2024 Bharat Future Fest 2026. H2O Theme & 10th Gen skilling.

"Education is not just about degrees, it's about creating opportunities. This fair has transformed into a career ecosystem."
— Dr. Sunita Verma, Chief Organizer

Preparation Guide

Preparation Tips & Notices

  • Carry 15+ printed resumes
  • Dress in formal attire
  • Research top 10 companies
  • Carry ID proof and certificates
  • Prepare 30-second self-introduction
  • Download event app for queue updates
  • Bring water bottle - H2O stations available

Registration Format: 

FairID: NCEF2026-XXXX
Example: NCEF2026-4572
QR Entry Code sent to email

Attendee Journey

  1. Register online and receive QR code
  2. Arrive at Gate 4 - Show QR
  3. Collect welcome kit
  4. Visit stalls and submit resume
  5. Attend workshops
  6. Submit feedback for certificate
