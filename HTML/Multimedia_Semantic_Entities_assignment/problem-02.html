Recruitment Stages

  1. Online Application Submission
  2. Initial Recruiter Screening
  3. Technical Assessment & Portfolio Review
  4. Panel & Culture-fit Interview
  5. Formal Offer & Onboarding

Stage Details

Screening
A brief 20-minute phone call with the HR team to discuss background, goals, and expectations.
Technical Round
An in-depth evaluation of domain skills, problem-solving, and practical technical tasks.
HR Round
A conversation focusing on organizational fit, culture alignment, values, and compensation expectations.
Offer
The formal job offer containing salary details, benefits, equity, and start date details.
