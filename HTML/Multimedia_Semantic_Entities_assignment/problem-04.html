Destination Overview: Kyoto, Japan

Welcome to Kyoto, the cultural heart of Japan – a city where ancient traditions seamlessly blend with modern life.

★ Travel Highlight: Cherry Blossom Season

Spring in Kyoto offers breathtaking views as sakura blossoms frame historic shrines and traditional wooden townhouses across the city.

One-Day Recommended Itinerary

  1. 07:30 AM – Arrive early at Fushimi Inari Shrine to avoid peak crowds.
  2. 10:30 AM – Take the local train to Kiyomizu-dera and stroll through Ninenzaka.
  3. 01:00 PM – Enjoy local street food and matcha ice cream at Nishiki Market.
  4. 03:30 PM – Visit Kinkaku-ji to witness the sun reflecting off the Golden Pavilion.
  5. 06:30 PM – Take a evening walk through Gion for dinner and traditional tea culture.
