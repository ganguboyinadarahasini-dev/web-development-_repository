Financial Mastery & Terminology

Essential Financial Tips

  • Follow the 50/30/20 rule: 50% needs, 30% wants, and 20% savings.
  • Automate monthly deposits into high-yield savings accounts.
  • Pay off high-interest credit card debt before investing in volatile assets.
  • Monitor your credit report at least once annually – free of cost.
  • Maintain an emergency fund covering 3 to 6 months of expenses.

Key Financial Terminology

Interest Rate
The percentage charged by lenders on borrowed principal or earned on deposited savings annually.
EMI (Equated Monthly Installment)
A fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month.
Principal
The original sum of money borrowed in a loan or put into an investment before interest accumulation.
Tenure
The total agreed period of time over which a loan must be repaid in full.

Account Opening Process

  1. Choose the financial product that aligns with your financial goals.
  2. Gather required documentation (Government ID, Address Proof, Proof of Income).
  3. Complete the online application form or request an educational callback.
  4. Verify your identity via e-KYC or at a local partner branch.
  5. Fund your account and set up digital online banking access.
