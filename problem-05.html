Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is made up of hydrogen and oxygen: H2O.

Carbon dioxide: CO2

Oxygen gas: O2

Hydrogen gas: H2

Sulfuric acid: H2SO4

Atoms are the basic units of matter.

Physics

Area is the amount of surface covered by an object.

Area of a square = s2

Volume of a cube = s3

Einstein's equation: E = mc2

Force is a push or pull acting on an object.

Biology

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their food.

Plants use chlorophyll to absorb light energy.

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms.

DNA carries genetic information in living organisms.

Living organisms require energy for growth and survival.
